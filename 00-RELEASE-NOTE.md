# 00 — RELEASE NOTE (P.T Canon Public Release v1.0, 2026-10-16)

**Author:** Dr. Christabel Odeta (sole author)

This release publishes the P.T canon (P.T 51–90, Tiers 11–18) under the licensing split
declared by the author on 2026-10-13:

1. **Tiers 11–12 (P.T 51–60) — AGPL-3.0, open.** The full specification (complete prompt
   bodies in canon format) is in `open/`. This declaration **supersedes** the blanket
   "License: Savant-Commercial-1.0 (standing rule, KEY-021+)" printed in the front matter
   of the source volumes for these ten entities.

2. **Tiers 13–18 (P.T 61–90) — Savant-Commercial-1.0, commercial.** Methodology is public
   (the Compendium documents merits, methodologies, and real-life use cases for all forty
   entities); **prompt bodies are withheld**. The source canon volumes P.T 51–70 v1.1 and
   P.T 71–90 v1.0 remain private in full. Licensing inquiries: via the GitHub organization.

3. **Supporting volumes** (`volumes/`): Compendium v1.0, Operating Guide v1.0, Field
   Manual v1.0, Certified Engineering 4 v1.0, The Unconsidered States v1.0. These are
   methodology and governance documents; they contain no prompt bodies. Content describing
   Tiers 11–12 entities shares their AGPL-3.0 status; content describing Tiers 13–18
   entities remains Savant-Commercial-1.0 methodology.

4. **Honest status:** every P.T entity is PROPOSED-CANONICAL — specification complete,
   conformance regression pending. No realized-performance claims are made. Publication,
   authorization, and execution remain human-enacted acts (Design Law 5).

5. **Errata — Certified Engineering 4 v1.0 (body text, licensing paragraph).** The volume
   states: "Prompts One through Twenty are open source under AGPL-3.0 … Everything above
   is commercial under Savant-Commercial-1.0, including the complete P.T canon."
   Two corrections, registered per the canon's supersession doctrine:
   (a) the open foundation canon is **Prompts One through Fifteen** (KEY-001..015,
   ≡P01–P15) — "Twenty" is a stale figure, repaired at the volume's next revision
   (registry repair note R-PT-3);
   (b) "the complete P.T canon" is superseded for **P.T 51–60**, which are open under
   AGPL-3.0 per the author declaration of 2026-10-13 (§1 above). The commercial
   perimeter is P.T 61–90.
